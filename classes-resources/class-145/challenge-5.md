# Code Challenge 5: Design the Entity-Relationship Diagram (ERD)

## Objective

In this challenge, you will apply the knowledge gained throughout our
database design module by creating a complete **Entity-Relationship
Diagram (ERD)** based on the **Third Normal Form (3NF)** of the
**Airline Reservation Management System**.

The relational model has already been designed and normalized. Your task
is to transform that relational model into a professional
Entity-Relationship Diagram that accurately represents the database
structure.

This activity will help you understand how database designers visualize
relational databases before implementation.

---

# Background

During the previous lessons, we analyzed the airline reservation
business requirements and progressively improved the database by
applying:

- First Normal Form (1NF)
- Second Normal Form (2NF)
- Third Normal Form (3NF)

The final relational model is now fully normalized and ready to be
represented graphically.

Your responsibility is to convert this relational model into a complete
Entity-Relationship Diagram.

---

# Your Task

Using the completed **Third Normal Form (3NF)** relational model,
design a professional Entity-Relationship Diagram (ERD).

The diagram must accurately represent every entity and every
relationship contained in the database.

---

# Your Diagram Must Include

## Entities

Include every entity from the final relational model.

Examples include:

- Passenger
- Phone
- Passenger Phone
- Pilot
- Pilot Phone
- Airport
- Aircraft
- Flight
- Reservation
- Ticket
- Flight Status
- Reservation Status
- Payment Status
- Etc

---

## Relationships

Draw every relationship between entities.

Every relationship must connect the appropriate primary and foreign
keys.

---

## Relationship Names

Label relationships whenever appropriate to improve readability.

Examples:

- Has
- Contains
- Assigned To
- Operates
- Generates
- Makes

---

# Design Requirements

Your ERD should be:

- Organized
- Easy to read
- Professionally structured
- Free of overlapping relationship lines
- Consistent in naming conventions

Arrange the entities logically so that related tables are grouped
together.

---

# Before Submitting

Verify that:

- Every entity is included.
- Every attribute is represented.
- Every primary key is identified.
- Every foreign key is identified.
- Every relationship is connected correctly.
- Every relationship has the correct cardinality.
- The diagram represents the complete Third Normal Form (3NF) database.

---

# Deliverables

Each student must submit:

- One complete Entity-Relationship Diagram (ERD).
- All entities.

---

# Evaluation Criteria

Your work will be evaluated according to the following criteria:

- Correct representation of all entities.
- Correct relationship design.
- Proper organization of the diagram.
- Professional presentation.
- Consistency with the Third Normal Form (3NF) relational model.

---

# Learning Outcomes

After completing this challenge, you should be able to:

- Interpret a normalized relational model.
- Transform a relational model into an Entity-Relationship Diagram.
- Identify entities and their attributes.
- Distinguish between primary and foreign keys.
- Represent relationships visually.
- Identify and apply relationship cardinalities.
- Produce professional database documentation suitable for software
  development projects.

This challenge simulates a common task performed by database analysts
and software engineers when documenting relational databases before they
are implemented in a Database Management System (DBMS) such as
PostgreSQL.
