**Status Definitions:**

- **Done:** The item satisfies its acceptance criteria and
  the Definition of Done.
- **In Progress:** Implementation has started, but the item
  does not satisfy all completion requirements.
- **Not Started:** Implementation has not started.
- **Blocked:** Work cannot continue due to an identified
  impediment or dependency.

**Important Considerations:**

- Only completed backlog items should count as completed
  Story Points.
- Partially implemented items must not be presented as
  completed increments.
- Incomplete items must return to the Product Backlog for
  prioritization and future consideration.
- The Product Owner is responsible for reviewing and
  accepting the delivered functionality.
